# ScamRing seed patterns: sources

Every source below was opened and read in full (fetched 3 October 2026). Scripts in `patterns.json` are paraphrased, with no real names, numbers, emails or account details.

| key | name | source | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| owner_abroad_keys_by_post | Owner abroad, keys sent by courier | [Daft.ie: Safety Online](https://support.daft.ie/hc/en-ie/articles/360003988977-Safety-Online) | Full example email from a "landlord" who moved abroad and will courier the keys after a deposit is paid. |
| mass_viewing_multiple_deposits | Fake host shows property to many, collects multiple deposits | [Garda: Student Fraud Warning, 25 Aug 2026](https://www.garda.ie/en/about-us/our-departments/office-of-corporate-communications/press-releases/2026/august/25-08-2026-an-garda-siochana-issues-student-fraud-warning-ahead-of-cao-offers-25th-august-2026.html) | Garda's "Fake Host/Mass Showing" category: deposits collected from many people, then the fraudster disappears. |
| pay_to_view_deposit | Small deposit to book a viewing, then fake bank call | [RTÉ: Fraudsters using fake rental ads to take money - AIB](https://rte.ie/news/business/2026/0402/1566446-fraud-rental-text/) | AIB Fraud Trend: a small deposit "to discourage no-shows", followed by a call pretending to be the victim's bank. Also covered by [TheJournal.ie](https://www.thejournal.ie/article.php?id=7000534). |
| cloned_site_payment_link | Link to a cloned listings/booking site to confirm card | [Limerick Post: TD warns of sophisticated scam targeting renters](https://www.limerickpost.ie/2026/02/12/limerick-td-warns-of-sophisticated-scam-targeting-limerick-renters) | Fake Daft-looking link, a 2 euro forfeitable viewing deposit, "Revolut not accepted", "do not refresh". [CCPC](https://www.ccpc.ie/consumers/housing/rental-accommodation-scams/) also warns about links to cloned payment sites. |
| sob_story_whatsapp_only | Landlord with a sob story, WhatsApp only, never available to meet | [Irish Examiner: The struggle to find student accommodation](https://www.irishexaminer.com/news/spotlight/arid-41696012.html) | 1,800 euro lost to a "deaf" landlady who used WhatsApp only and kept putting off viewings because of a family event. Daft also flags sob stories. |
| id_documents_upfront | Requests ID copy plus deposit before any meeting | [Daft.ie: Safety Online](https://support.daft.ie/hc/en-ie/articles/360003988977-Safety-Online) | "Email deposit scam": deposit and rent before meeting, keys promised, and ID requested that is later used for other fraud. |
| hijacked_listing_new_contact | Genuine listing re-advertised with scammer's contact details | [BPFI / FraudSMART: Students warned of rental scams](https://bpfi.ie/students-warned-rental-accommodation-scams-ahead-leaving-cert-results/) | Real listings re-advertised with the fraudster's own email or phone, no viewings, photos or keys sent once money is paid. |
| untraceable_payment_method | Insists on cash, crypto, Revolut or a foreign bank account | [Garda: Student Accommodation Fraud Warning, Aug 2025](https://www.garda.ie/en/about-us/our-departments/office-of-corporate-communications/press-releases/2025/august/an-garda-siochana-student-accommodation-fraud-warning-august-2025.html) | Red flags: cash, crypto, non-bank or wire transfer, a foreign account. The release advises against Revolut, gift cards and Western Union. |
| move_off_platform_discount | Discount to book or pay off the platform | [Travel Extra quoting Bank of Ireland](https://www.travelextra.ie/dont-be-tempted-to-move-off-the-website-and-book-direct-increase-in-holiday-accommodation-fraud-noticed-by-bank/) | Bank of Ireland has seen "owners" offer discounts to move off Airbnb or Booking.com, and those bookings turned out to be fraud. |
| urgency_deposit_before_lease | Pressure to pay deposit and rent now, before any lease | [CCPC: Rental accommodation scams](https://www.ccpc.ie/consumers/housing/rental-accommodation-scams/) | Pressure to pay or lose the property, deposit and rent before a lease, no questions asked of the tenant. |
